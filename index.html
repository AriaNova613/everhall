Double ⤫ Cross

A little daily crossword with a crooked heart.

1
Every clue has two answers.

“Big cat (4)” might be LION and PUMA. Each clue owns two twin slots of the same length — mirror images across the grid. The clue never says which answer lives where.

2
Pencil freely. The grid is your witness.

Type letters anywhere — pencil is never judged. Placement is proved by the crossings: if a crossing pair writes a U into a square either way round, whatever crosses it must agree. The ⇄ button flips a pair between its two homes without retyping.

Both homes of 2-Down put a U in that square → 1-Across must be PUMA, not LION.
3
Fill the whole grid, then SEAL it. Once.

The seal judges everything at the end. Right everywhere — the day is yours. Wrong — a lamp lights, true entries flash green, wrong ones flash red and stay softly underlined until you rework them. The fourth lamp ends the day.

Stuck? The 💡 chisel buys one true letter for a lamp. The 📖 almanac settles a pair of your choosing free — one page a day, another when a seal breaks — but the day is marked assisted.

Seal true on your very first press, unaided: ✦ Flawless.

The rules have deepened

The locks are gone. Now you pencil the whole grid, then press SEAL — once, at the end.

⤫

A wrong seal lights a lamp: true entries flash green, wrong ones flash red and stay underlined until you rework them. Four lamps still end the day. The 💡 chisel and 📖 almanac are still here when you need them.

Your streak and every stat carry on exactly as they are — and your first stumble under the new rules is on the house.
